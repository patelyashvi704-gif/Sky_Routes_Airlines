======================================================================
SKYROUTES AIRLINES - ROUTE PROFITABILITY ANALYSIS INSIGHTS
======================================================================

1. High-Cost Aircraft Types:
   Aircraft models with lower seat capacities running on long-duration routes operate on negative profit margins due to excessive operational costs per seat.

2. High Revenue vs Low Profit Routes:
   Certain international routes generate peak top-line revenue, but elevated fuel and operational costs severely diminish overall profit margins.

3. Duration & Distance Impact:
   Short-to-medium haul domestic flights demonstrate higher profit margins per flight minute than long-haul international flights.

4. Demand Trends:
   Monthly analysis highlights seasonal profit spikes during mid-year holiday periods, while off-season months suffer low seat occupancy and cost overhangs.

5. Strategic Recommendation:
   Reallocate low-occupancy aircraft to high-demand domestic corridors and adjust ticket pricing structure on underperforming international routes.
